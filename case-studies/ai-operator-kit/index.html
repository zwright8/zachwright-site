Case study / AI Operator Kit

Turning a product bundle into an inspectable operating system.

AI Operator Kit is a repo-visible package for weekly operating cadence, delivery QA, staged automation, and tiered team rollout. This page explains the system decisions visible in the shipped artifacts rather than relying on generic portfolio copy.

What this proves

Product judgment backed by files.

The artifact package shows how Zach turns advisory knowledge into a bounded, versioned product surface with operational safeguards.

Audience

Solo operators, small teams, and agencies

Tier docs separate simple weekly execution from multi-client team rollout.

System

SOPs, dashboard cadence, prompts, and automation checklist

The package makes manual process stability a prerequisite for automation.

Trust

Version, license, refund, and include documents

Customer-facing boundaries are explicit instead of hidden in sales copy.

Quality

Agency QA scorecards and rollout templates

Delivery quality is reviewed with scoring, failure tags, and training actions.

System shape

A package that moves from intake to safer automation.

The bundle is designed as an operating sequence. Each stage leaves a concrete file a reviewer can open and evaluate.

1

Pick a working surface

START-HERE tells users to pick one offer or service before customizing the system.

2

Install operating cadence

The SOP library defines owners, triggers, inputs, procedures, QA checks, and escalation rules.

3

Track weekly decisions

The dashboard template turns sales, delivery, and backlog signals into reviewable priorities.

4

Automate after stability

The automation checklist explicitly comes after manual workflow foundations are working.

5

Score delivery quality

Agency scorecards add shipping thresholds, recurring failure tags, and owner training notes.

Engineering decisions

Constraints are visible in the artifact.

No fake proof

Benchmark claims were removed in v2.

The version history says the package removed implied benchmark claims and default performance metrics, keeping the product grounded in inspectable workflows.

Staged automation

Automation follows operational clarity.

The setup order places SOPs and dashboard rhythm before prompt packs and automation checklists, reducing the risk of automating unstable process.

Tiered packaging

Different buyers get different operating depth.

Lite, Pro, and Agency include docs separate solo execution, small-team operations, and multi-client enablement without changing the core mental model.

Deploy artifact

Static product files ship with the portfolio build.

The Vite copy hook keeps product files, updates, research, assets, and dashboard surfaces in the production artifact.

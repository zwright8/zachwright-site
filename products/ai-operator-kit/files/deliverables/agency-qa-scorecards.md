# AI Operator Kit — Agency QA Scorecards

Score every deliverable before client send.

## Scorecard (0–2 each)
1. Scope adherence
2. Actionability
3. Evidence/logic quality
4. Readability
5. Formatting/professional polish

### Total scoring
- 9–10: Ship
- 7–8: Minor fixes then ship
- <=6: Rework required

## Failure pattern tags
- `scope-drift`
- `missing-next-step`
- `insufficient-detail`
- `formatting-noise`
- `owner-mismatch`

## Weekly QA review template
- Top 3 recurring issues
- Root causes
- SOP updates needed
- Owner training actions

# AI Operator Kit — SOP Library (v2)

These SOPs are designed as practical templates. Customize owners, tools, and response times to your business.

## SOP 1 — Lead Intake & Qualification

**Purpose:** Ensure every inbound lead receives a consistent, timely first action.

**Trigger:** New lead arrives via form, DM, referral, or email.

**Owner:** Sales Ops (or founder if solo)

**Inputs:** Lead source, contact info, notes, service interest

**Procedure:**
1. Log lead in your CRM/intake tracker.
2. Capture required fields: source, problem, budget signal (if known), urgency, decision-maker.
3. Score fit against ICP criteria (High/Medium/Low).
4. Route by fit:
   - High: invite to discovery
   - Medium: send qualification questions
   - Low: send polite decline or resource response
5. Set a follow-up task with owner and due date.

**QA checklist:**
- [ ] Lead record complete
- [ ] Fit score documented
- [ ] Next action assigned
- [ ] Follow-up message sent

**Escalation rule:** Escalate to founder when lead is strategic, high-value, or outside normal scope.

## SOP 2 — Discovery to Proposal

**Purpose:** Convert qualified discovery calls into clear proposals with minimal rework.

**Trigger:** Discovery call completed.

**Owner:** Account lead / founder

**Inputs:** Call notes, constraints, goals, budget/timeline expectations

**Procedure:**
1. Summarize client objective and current bottleneck.
2. Define in-scope and out-of-scope items.
3. Draft tiered options (if applicable) with meaningful tradeoffs.
4. Add assumptions, dependencies, and client responsibilities.
5. Share proposal with explicit next step and decision date.
6. Schedule follow-up task in CRM.

**QA checklist:**
- [ ] Scope is specific
- [ ] Boundaries are clear
- [ ] Timeline and milestones included
- [ ] Pricing and terms included
- [ ] Next-step CTA included

**Escalation rule:** Escalate if requested scope is materially different from the offer.

## SOP 3 — Client Onboarding

**Purpose:** Start projects cleanly and reduce delays in week one.

**Trigger:** Proposal accepted and payment/contract complete.

**Owner:** Delivery Ops / project lead

**Inputs:** Signed agreement, statement of work, client contacts

**Procedure:**
1. Send onboarding welcome with timeline and required inputs.
2. Create project workspace (folders, PM board, communication channel).
3. Assign internal roles and first milestone owners.
4. Book kickoff meeting.
5. Confirm decision-maker and feedback workflow.

**QA checklist:**
- [ ] Project workspace created
- [ ] Kickoff scheduled
- [ ] Required client inputs requested
- [ ] Internal owners assigned

**Escalation rule:** Escalate when client dependencies are blocked beyond agreed window.

## SOP 4 — Weekly Delivery Review

**Purpose:** Keep delivery on track and identify risks early.

**Trigger:** Weekly team review cadence.

**Owner:** Project lead

**Inputs:** Milestone status, blockers, client requests, QA notes

**Procedure:**
1. Review milestone status (on-track / at-risk / off-track).
2. Identify blockers and root causes.
3. Confirm next-week priorities and owners.
4. Prepare client-facing update.
5. Log scope changes separately (see change-control SOP).

**QA checklist:**
- [ ] Status updated in PM system
- [ ] Blockers have owners
- [ ] Client update prepared
- [ ] Next-week priorities assigned

## SOP 5 — Change Request & Scope Control

**Purpose:** Protect delivery quality and margin when scope changes.

**Trigger:** Client requests work outside current scope.

**Owner:** Account lead + project lead

**Inputs:** Original scope, requested change, timeline impact

**Procedure:**
1. Document requested change in writing.
2. Assess impact on timeline, resources, and deliverables.
3. Decide path:
   - Include as minor adjustment
   - Move to future phase
   - Issue paid change order
4. Get written client approval before starting change.
5. Update project plan and communicate revised expectations.

**QA checklist:**
- [ ] Change documented
- [ ] Impact assessed
- [ ] Approval recorded
- [ ] Plan updated

## SOP 6 — Pre-Delivery QA

**Purpose:** Prevent avoidable errors before client delivery.

**Trigger:** Deliverable ready for handoff.

**Owner:** Delivery owner + reviewer

**Inputs:** Final draft, source files, requirements checklist

**Procedure:**
1. Validate against deliverable requirements.
2. Verify factual and formatting accuracy.
3. Perform peer review (if team size allows).
4. Resolve issues and re-check.
5. Deliver with concise summary of what is included.

**QA checklist:**
- [ ] Requirements matched
- [ ] Accuracy reviewed
- [ ] Formatting consistent
- [ ] Final approval recorded

## SOP 7 — Customer Support & Refund Handling

**Purpose:** Handle support and refund requests consistently and professionally.

**Trigger:** Customer issue or refund request received.

**Owner:** Support owner / founder

**Inputs:** Request details, order details, policy docs

**Procedure:**
1. Acknowledge receipt.
2. Categorize issue (billing, access, quality, other).
3. Check against policy and order history.
4. Decide: resolve, refund, partial remedy, or escalate.
5. Respond with clear action and timeline.
6. Log cause and prevention note.

**QA checklist:**
- [ ] Request categorized
- [ ] Policy check completed
- [ ] Decision documented
- [ ] Customer response sent

## SOP 8 — Weekly Operator Planning

**Purpose:** Translate business signals into focused weekly execution.

**Trigger:** End-of-week review / start-of-week planning.

**Owner:** Founder / operator

**Inputs:** Dashboard metrics, sales pipeline, delivery status, backlog

**Procedure:**
1. Review what moved vs what stalled.
2. Identify bottlenecks and key decisions.
3. Set top 3 priorities for next week.
4. Assign owners and deadlines.
5. Remove low-leverage tasks from active plan.

**QA checklist:**
- [ ] Priorities are specific
- [ ] Owners assigned
- [ ] Deadlines set
- [ ] Team informed

# Refund Policy (7-Day)

We offer a simple **7-day refund window** from the date of purchase.

## Eligible refunds

You are eligible for a refund if you request it within 7 calendar days of purchase.

## How to request

Send your refund request with:
- Purchase email
- Date of purchase
- Product name

## Processing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method.
Processing time depends on your payment provider.

## Notes

- Requests made after 7 days are not eligible.
- By purchasing, you agree to this policy.

The Big Thing

OpenAI made a two-front power move: a $110B funding round to scale infrastructure, and a classified cloud deployment agreement with the U.S. Department of War.

Why it matters: this is the clearest signal yet that frontier AI competition is now capital + government alignment + deployment architecture, not just model quality.

  • OpenAI funding announcement ($110B at $730B pre-money): https://openai.com/index/scaling-ai-for-everyone/
  • OpenAI DoW agreement + guardrails details: https://openai.com/index/our-agreement-with-the-department-of-war/
  • Anthropic response to “supply chain risk” comments: https://www.anthropic.com/news/statement-comments-secretary-war
  • TechCrunch funding breakdown/context: https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/27/openai-raises-110b-in-one-of-the-largest-private-funding-rounds-in-history/

Tech Impact

  • Energy risk just re-entered the chat. Reuters reported Brent jumping ~10% OTC, with analysts warning $100 oil is plausible if Hormuz disruption persists.
    https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-jumps-10-iran-conflict-could-spike-100-barrel-analysts-say-2026-03-01/
  • Shipping chokepoint stress is now observable, not hypothetical. Reuters reported at least 150 tankers anchored outside Hormuz and broader vessel gridlock.
    https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/hundreds-ships-drop-anchor-middle-east-gulf-us-war-iran-escalates-data-shows-2026-03-01/
  • OPEC+ responded, but only with a modest cushion. Output was raised by 206k bpd, while analysts still flagged limited spare capacity and continued route sensitivity.
    https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/opec-debates-oil-output-boost-us-war-iran-disrupts-shipments-2026-03-01/
  • Geopolitics is now directly setting AI market conditions. Security alignment, shipping volatility, and compute-capex concentration are converging into the same risk surface.

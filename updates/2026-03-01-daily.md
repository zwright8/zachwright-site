## The Big Thing
**OpenAI made a two-front power move: a $110B funding round to scale infrastructure, and a classified cloud deployment agreement with the U.S. Department of War.**

Why it matters: this is the clearest signal yet that frontier AI competition is now **capital + government alignment + deployment architecture**, not just model quality.

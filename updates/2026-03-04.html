The Big Thing

AI strategy is splitting in two at once: open-source acceleration at the edge, centralized power fights at the top.

Why it matters: distribution and pricing are now as important as raw model quality. Teams that can ship fast + cheap win mindshare, while policy fights decide who gets to serve government and enterprise demand.

  • Google launched Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite at roughly 1/8 the cost of Pro, pushing the cost floor lower for production AI workloads. https://venturebeat.com/technology/google-releases-gemini-3-1-flash-lite-at-1-8th-the-cost-of-pro
  • OpenAI is reportedly developing a GitHub rival, signaling tighter vertical integration around coding workflows. https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence

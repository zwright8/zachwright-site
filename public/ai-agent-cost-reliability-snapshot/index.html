Fixed boundaries

The snapshot measures supplied evidence—not your organization.

Accepted

  • One workflow

  • Up to 50 normalized attempts
  • Prompt-free JSONL or CSV
  • Supplied outcome and cost evidence

Never accepted

  • Prompts, responses, tool payloads, or results
  • Credentials, direct identifiers, or production access

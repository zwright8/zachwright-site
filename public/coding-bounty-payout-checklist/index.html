Decision rule

Missing funding is not a small detail.

If reserved funding, acceptance authority, or a usable payout path cannot be verified, the rational state is HOLD. Do not manufacture a payment probability to make the expected value look positive.

See the $49 evidence review ↗
  1. 01

    Funding exists now

    An advertised reward is marketing. Escrow, a reserved processor balance, or job-specific on-chain funds are evidence.

  2. 02

    Authority is explicit

    Name who can accept delivery, reject it, release payment, resolve disputes, and change the deadline.

  3. 03

    The paid case survives costs

    Subtract known fees and divide by bounded labor hours. Call that paid-case hourly return—not expected value.

  4. 04

    Unknown stays unknown

    Competition, asset liquidity, rights, or acceptance gaps do not become favorable assumptions because the headline reward is large.

  5. 05

    Worker-funded claims stop

    A required deposit, bond, purchase, transfer, or trade changes a zero-capital job into a spend decision and a separate risk.

  6. 06

    Scope stays ordinary

    This checklist is for lawful software, documentation, data, design, and integration work—not vulnerability research or security testing.

Two public listings advertised $250 and $149 from the same buyer. The buyer address held 0.084841 USDC, the platform said there was no escrow and payment followed delivery, public legal routes were absent, and prior completed records exposed no payout proof.

  1. 01

    Headline reward

    $399 across two listings looked attractive before funding was checked.

  2. 02

    Buyer funds

    0.084841 USDC could not fund either advertised job.

  3. 03

    Delivery protection

    No escrow meant the worker would deliver before any documented settlement.

  4. 04

    Decision

    NO-GO. A proposal would not repair missing funds, legal allocation, or payout history.

Common decisions

Direct answers.

Do I need a GitHub account?

No GitHub account required. The worksheet runs entirely in this page; only the optional paid-review intake offers GitHub and business-email routes.

Is an on-chain balance the same as escrow?

No. A wallet balance may show capacity, but it does not prove that funds are reserved for your job or that you can release them. Verify the exact payment mechanism.

What if every check passes except acceptance criteria?

HOLD. A funded reward without an objective passing condition can still become unpaid work.

Does GO guarantee payment?

No. GO means the public evidence clears the stated gates at the cutoff. It is not a payout, profit, or outcome guarantee.

Does the worksheet save my bounty details?

No. It has no submission endpoint, analytics call, storage API, cookie, or account requirement. Resetting or closing the page clears the inputs.

Can this be used for bug bounties?

No. WrightOps does not provide vulnerability research, exploitation, security testing, or security-bounty guidance. This page is for ordinary software work.
